    Moving in Water

    The students are able to assess situations in, on, and around water in terms of safety and behave appropriately. The focus is on swimming safely. In addition to learning swimming techniques, they acquire selected competencies in other aquatic activities such as diving, underwater swimming, and lifesaving.

    Swimming

    The students can swim safely. They know technical characteristics of different swimming techniques and apply them.

    Swimming

    • First Cycle:
      • Move and play freely in chest-deep water
      • Apply the core elements of breathing, floating, gliding, and propulsion in various situations
      • Stay afloat in place for one minute
    • Second Cycle:
      • Swim 50 meters in a self-chosen technique
      • Apply core movements of back crawl and front crawl
      • Apply core movements of breaststroke
      • Name and apply key features of one alternating and one simultaneous stroke over 50 meters
    • Third Cycle:
      • Swim 100 meters in a self-chosen technique
      • Swim a long distance using a freely chosen technique
      • Name and apply key features of various swimming techniques
      • Apply techniques from other aquatic sports

    Jumping into Water and Diving

    The students can jump into the water feet- and head-first and dive.

    Jumping into Water and Diving

    • First Cycle:
      • Jump feet-first into chest-deep water
      • Stay underwater briefly and exhale while submerged
      • Jump into deep water and submerge completely
      • Keep their eyes open underwater and retrieve objects in chest-deep water
    • Second Cycle:
      • Roll into deep water
      • Dive a short distance with few strokes
    • Third Cycle:
      • Dive head-first into deep water and reach a depth greater than their height
      • Perform various jumps from different heights
      • Equalize pressure when diving to depth

    Security in the Water

    The students can assess situations in, near, and on the water with regard to safety and act responsibly in dangerous situations.

    Security

    • First Cycle:
      • Name risks in, near, and on the water
      • Recognize dangerous situations and follow rules under supervision
    • Second Cycle:
      • Realistically assess and avoid dangerous situations
      • Follow swimming and diving safety rules
      • Act responsibly in dangerous situations
    • Third Cycle:
      • Remind others to follow swimming and diving safety rules
      • Act responsibly toward themselves and others
      • Name causes of drowning

    Alarming and Rescuing

    • First Cycle:
      • Raise the alarm on instruction
    • Second Cycle:
      • Recognize when a person is in distress and raise the alarm
    • Third Cycle:
      • Recognize emergencies and take appropriate action
      • Apply basic rescue techniques
