    Sliding, Rolling, Riding

    The students expand their skills in gliding, rolling, and riding, which particularly enhances their sense of balance. Depending on local conditions, they gain experience on bicycles, inline skates, ice skates, skis or snowboards. The skills and abilities they acquire can be transferred to extracurricular sports activities. Aspects of safety and sustainability are promoted, encouraging a responsible approach to both people and nature.

    Sliding, Rolling and Riding

    The students can slide, roll, and ride responsibly on various surfaces.

    Rolling and Riding

    • First Cycle:
      • Ride on wheeled devices in a protected and safe environment
      • Avoid obstacles and brake safely on wheeled devices
    • Second Cycle:
      • Ride a bicycle safely on the road
    • Third Cycle:
      • Move on wheeled devices in a way that suits the situation

    Sliding

    • First Cycle:
      • Slide in various body positions
      • Slide in a controlled manner using sliding devices
    • Second Cycle:
      • Move by using sliding devices
      • Perform basic movements on sliding devices
    • Third Cycle:
      • Vary basic movements on sliding devices

    Security and Responsibility

    • First Cycle:
      • Move safely in nature in different weather and surface conditions
      • Follow given safety rules
      • Recognize dangerous situations
    • Second Cycle:
      • Realistically assess abilities and understand the importance of protective equipment
      • Name and follow safety guidelines
      • Assess dangerous situations and act accordingly
    • Third Cycle:
      • Apply strategies to avoid danger and know how to act in emergencies
      • Act responsibly toward nature, others, and themselves
