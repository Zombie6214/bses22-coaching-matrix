    Playing

    Through playful activities, the students experience joy and excitement in playing. They explore a variety of games, develop their own game ideas and agree on rules for playing together. The learn to cope with winning and losing. In both cooperative and competitive settings, students improve tactical and technical skills that are applicable across different types of games or sports. Through competition, they learn to follow established rules and rituals and to show respect for others. These situations provide opportunities to address emotional awareness, strengthen conflict resolution skills, and promote aspects of fair play.

    Movement Games

    Students can play, further develop, and invent games by making and following agreements together.

    Playing, Developing and Inventing

    • First Cycle:
      • Recognize given roles in games
      • Participate in games in various roles and follow the rules
      • Take on different roles during a game and act accordingly
    • Second Cycle:
      • Modify games in terms of rules, playing field, game object, team, and roles, and play independently
      • Recognize and point out fair behavior and rule violations in themselves and others
    • Third Cycle:
      • Further develop, invent, and play games independently and fairly
      • Address and resolve conflicts in games constructively

    Sport Games

    The students can apply technical and tactical action patterns in various sports. They know the rules, can play independently and fairly, and reflect on emotions.

    Receiving and Passing

    • First Cycle:
      • Receive and pass objects
      • Receive and pass a ball or other game object while running
      • Keep the ball or game object in play in small group situations
    • Second Cycle Focus Area:
      • Receive and pass the ball or game object in small team games
      • Receive and pass the ball or game object in simplified team sports
    • Third Cycle:
      • Receive and pass the ball or game object appropriately in game situations

    Leading Balls or Objects

    • First Cycle:
      • Control the ball or game object
      • Control the ball or game object next to others
    • Second Cycle:
      • Control the ball or game object in small games
    • Third Cycle:
      • Control the ball or game object effectively in team sports
      • Dribble the ball or game object and use feints

    Target Aiming

    • First Cycle:
      • Hit a target from a standing position
      • Hit a target while running
    • Second Cycle:
      • Hit a target during a game
    • Third Cycle:
      • Hit a target despite pressure from opponents

    Tactics

    • First Cycle:
      • Recognize the movements of teammates and opponents and respond accordingly
      • Offer as a passing option and pass to teammates
    • Second Cycle:
      • Recognize the path of the ball or object and identify open space
      • Position correctly in defense and protect open space
    • Third Cycle:
      • Apply tactical action patterns in team sports
      • Analyze game situations and find solutions

    Rules

    • First Cycle:
      • Name rules
      • Follow rules
      • Apply rules while playing in small groups
    • Second Cycle:
      • Respect teammates and opponents and play without a referee for short periods
    • Third Cycle:
      • Explain important rules of sports, play independently and fairly
      • Address and resolve conflicts during play

    Emotions

    • First Cycle:
      • Perceive own emotions
      • Express own emotions and recognize the emotions of others
    • Second Cycle:
      • Reflect on emotions with guidance
      • Control emotions
    • Third Cycle:
      • Reflect on emotions independently
      • Manage emotions consciously

    Combat Games

    The students can fight skillfully, strategically, and fairly.

    Combat

    • Second Cycle:
      • Deliberately throw their opponent off balance
      • Perceive and respond to the opponent’s movements in combat games
      • Roll forwards and backwards to avoid injuries
    • Third Cycle:
      • Use strength and strategy purposefully in combat games

    Rules

    • First Cycle:
      • Accept physical contact
      • Recognize and set stop signals
      • Name and follow rituals and rules in combat games
    • Second Cycle:
      • Respect the opponent in combat games
      • Name dangerous actions and refrain from using them
    • Third Cycle:
      • Fight fairly without a referee and without injuring the opponent
