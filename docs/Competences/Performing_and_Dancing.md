<h1 style="font-weight: 500; color: var(--md-primary-fg-color)">Performing and Dancing</h1>

The students are able to move in harmony with rhythm and music, enhancing their perception. They expand and deepen their abilities in rhythm, creative movement, and expression, and they can perform movement arts using various materials. A respectful attitude toward themselves and others fosters constructive group dynamics. They learn to present themselves confidently through performances. 

## Body Awareness
The students can perceive their body, control it deliberately, and correct themselves during movement execution.

### Body Awareness
- First Cycle:
    - Perceive the environment and themselves using various senses and distinguish and name body parts
    - Perceive the position of the body in space
    - Deliberately control body parts
- Second Cycle:
    - Understand and follow movement instructions
    - Isolate and control movements of individual body parts
    - Pay attention to the quality of movement and posture
- Third Cycle:
    - Feel themselves during movement execution and make corrections
    - Develop an understanding of the connection between control and movement quality

## Performing and Creating
 The students can express themselves with their body and materials, choreograph a movement sequence, and present it.

### Performance
 - First Cycle:
    - Move to songs, verses, and images, and creatively interpret their content through improvisation
    - Imitate movements and experience themselves in different roles
    - Express feelings and find own movements to match them
- Second Cycle:
    - Combine movements and design them expressively
    - Vary and design a movement sequence according to the criteria of space, time, and energy
- Third Cycle:
    - Choreograph and present a movement sequence 

### Movement Skills
 - First Cycle:
    - Move an object according to its properties
    - Throw and catch an object with both the right and left hand
    - Perform movement patterns using various materials
- Second Cycle:
    - Perform movement tricks with materials
    - Perform a sequence of movement tricks
- Third Cycle:
    - Choreograph and present a sequence of movement tricks
    - Teach movement tricks

## Dancing
 The students can recognize movement patterns, design and reproduce movement sequences and dances to music rhytmically. The treat each other with respect.

### Rhytmical Movement
 - First Cycle:
    - Adapt their movements to the music
    - Move with the beat
- Second Cycle:
    - Move rhythmically to accented music
- Third Cycle:
    - Interpret different time signatures and musical styles rhythmically
    - Recognize the structure of music and create their own movement sequence accordingly

### Dancing
 - First Cycle:
    - Move in contrasting ways
    - Move expressively in various dance styles
- Second Cycle:
    - Perform dance-specific movements considering spatial pathways
    - Combine dance-specific movement patterns into movement sequences and perform them
    - Recognize and dance movement patterns from various dance styles
- Third Cycle:
    - Memorize and present dance choreographies
    - Use the body as a means of expression, performance, and communication

### Respectful Behaviour
 - First Cycle:
    - Move in a group and behave respectfully
    - Appreciate the own movement expression
- Second Cycle:
    - Respectfully acknowledge the movement expression of others
- Third Cycle:
    - Interact respectfully during creative processes
