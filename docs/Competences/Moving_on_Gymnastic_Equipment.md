<h1 style="font-weight: 500; color: var(--md-primary-fg-color)">Moving on Gymnastic Equipment</h1>

The students gain experience in dealing with gravity and improve their strength and flexibility. They expand their movement repertoire using various gymnastic equipment and learn to perform movements in a coordinated and harmonious way. The students learn to assess risks and overcome fears. Collaborative movement activities, as well as assisting and ensuring safety, contribute to the development of their cooperation skills.

## Basic Movements on Gymnastic Equipment
The students can perform basic movement such as balancing, rolling-turning, swinging, jumping, supporting, and climbing responsibly. They know the quality criteria and are able to assist and ensure safety for one another.

### Balance
- First Cycle:
    - Balance on a narrow surface
    - Balance on a narrow surface in various ways
    - Balance on unstable equipment
- Second Cycle:
    - Balance on equipment under challenging conditions
    - Perform a sequence of movements for balancing
- Third Cycle:
    - Balance on difficult and challenging equipment

### Rolling and Turning
- First Cycle:
    - Roll and turn on an inclined Surface
    - Perform a forward roll
    - Roll and turn on various apparatus
- Second Cycle:
    - Perform rolling and turning movements
    - Perform a sequence of rolling and turning movements
- Third Cycle:
    - Perform rolling and turning movements under challenging conditions
    - Perform rolling or turning movements with a flight phase in a controlled manner

### Swinging
- First Cycle:
    - Swing on and off different pieces of equipment
    - Perceive the turning point during swinging and pendulum movements
    - Swing rhythmically on the rings
- Second Cycle:
    - Enhance swinging movements by adding elements
- Third Cycle:
    - Perform a sequence of swinging or pendulum movements.
    - Perform a sequence of movements under challenging conditions

### Springing, Supporting and Climbing
- First Cycle:
    - Move on apparatus by supporting themselves and swinging
    - Land with control
    - Jump off a springboard with both feet and land with control
    - Overcome various obstacles in a sequence by supporting themselves and climbing
- Second Cycle:
    - Jump powerfully off the springboard, perform movements in the air, and land with control
    - Efficiently overcome a sequence of obstacles
- Third Cycle:
    - Vary the way of overcoming obstacles
    - Perform overhead support jumps

### Risk and Responsibility
- First Cycle:
    - Recognize risky situations and name the emotions
    - Reflect on risky situations with guidance
- Second Cycle:
    - Realistically assess themselves in risky situations
    - Act responsibly in risky situations
- Third Cycle:
    - Act responsibly towards others and themselves

### Supporting, Securing and Cooperating
- First Cycle:
    - Lead each other
    - Carry each other correctly and safely
    - Name equipment, transport it correctly, and set it up according to a plan
- Second Cycle:
    - Help and spot each other during movement tasks
    - Apply spotting techniques and safety measures appropriately to the situation
- Third Cycle:
    - Create and present movement sequences on apparatus combinations in groups

## Flexibility, Strength and Bodytension
The students can build body tension, support their body, and move their joints through a functional range of motion. They know how to train flexibility and strength.

### Flexibilty and Strength
- First Cycle:
    - Perceive the range of motion of their joints
    - Utilize the range of motion of their joints and support their body
    - Support their tense body on their hands
- Second Cycle:
    - Maintain and improve flexibility and strength under guidance
- Third Cycle:
    - Explain and apply training principles for improving flexibility and increasing strength
    - Independently train flexibility and strength

### Body Tension
- First Cycle:
    - Perceive the body as relaxed and tense
    - Tense the body in static I-poses
- Second Cycle:
    - Purposefully apply the core poses in movement
- Third Cycle:
    - Tense and relax their body at the right moments during movement sequences
