##  [Running, Jumping, Throwing](../Competences/Running_Jumping_Throwing.md)
### Fast Running
- Identify and apply important features of sprinting technique
- Run fast on the balls of the feet over low obstacles

### Endurance Running
- Adjust their running speed during a prolonged run
- Run for as many minutes as their age and understand that regular training is crucial for improving performance

### Orientation
- Navigate the school grounds while running using a map
- Navigate in the school community while running using a map

### Rhytmical Jumping
- Perform various tricks with the jump rope

### Long Jumping
- Cover a distance using multiple jumps
- Identify key elements of long jump technique and perform take-offs and jumps for distance using both the right and left leg
- Convert their approach speed into a long jump

### High Jumping
- Jump vertically with an upright torso and a clear swinging leg action, taking off with both the right and left leg
- Identify key elements of vertical jumping, take off with both the right and left leg, and perform a high jump technique

### Throwing
- Identify and aplly key elements of the throwing technique with a three-step run-up
- Perform a sling throw from a standing position

- Perform a sequence of movement tricks

### Rhytmical Movement
- Move rhythmically to accented music

### Dancing
- Perform dance-specific movements considering spatial pathways

## [Playing ](../Competences/Playing.md)
### Playing, Developing and Inventing
- Modify games in terms of rules, playing field, game object, team, and roles, and play independently
- Recognize and point out fair behavior and rule violations in themselves and others

### Receiving and Passing
- Receive and pass the ball or game object in small team games
- Receive and pass the ball or game object in simplified team sports

### Leading Balls or Objects
- Control the ball or game object in small games

### Target Aiming
- Hit a target during a game

### Tactics
- Recognize the path of the ball or object and identify open space
- Position correctly in defense and protect open space

### Rules
- Respect teammates and opponents and play without a referee for short periods

### Emotions
- Reflect on emotions with guidance
- Control emotions

### Combat
- Deliberately throw their opponent off balance
- Perceive and respond to the opponent’s movements in combat games
- Roll forwards and backwards to avoid injuries

### Rules
- Respect the opponent in combat games
- Name dangerous actions and refrain from using them

## [Moving in Water](../Competences/Moving_in_Water.md)
### Swimming
- Swim 50 meters in a self-chosen technique
- Apply core movements of back crawl and front crawl
- Apply core movements of breaststroke
- Name and apply key features of one alternating and one simultaneous stroke over 50 meters

### Jumping into Water and Diving
- Roll into deep water
- Dive a short distance with few strokes

### Security
- Realistically assess and avoid dangerous situations
- Follow swimming and diving safety rules
- Act responsibly in dangerous situations

### Alarming and Rescuing
- Recognize when a person is in distress and raise the alarm
