##  [Running, Jumping, Throwing](../Competences/Running_Jumping_Throwing.md)
### Fast Running
- Run fast and rhytmic on the balls of the feet over obstacles
- Apply important features of sprinting techniques when running hurdles

### Endurance Running
- Run for as many minutes as their age. They can explain how endurance is trained and understand the processes that occur in the body
- Explain and perform different endurance training methods and justify their performance development

### Orientation
- Follow a route using a map in terrain or forest areas
- Explain what to pay attention to when using a map for navigation and apply this knowledge in orienteering

### Rhytmical Jumping
- Combine different hopping and jumping sequences, both with and without equipment
- Teach jumps and tricks to classmates

### Long Jumping
- Apply key elements of long jump technique
- Observe key elements of long jump technique in classmates and provide feedback

### High Jumping
- Apply key elements of a high jump technique and realistically assess their own performance
- Observe key elements of high jump technique in their classmates and provide feedback

### Throwing
- Identify and aplly key elements of the throwing technique with a five-step run-up
- Hurl an object using a rotational movement
- Observe key elements of throwing and hurling in classmates and provide feedback
- Identify and aplly key elements of javelin throwing or rotational throwing

### Pushing
- Push an object for distance using full-body extension
- Identify key elements of shot put technique and apply them during the push
- Push a shot put far and observe key technique elements in classmates and provide feedback

## [Moving on Gymnastic Equipment](../Competences/Moving_on_Gymnastic_Equipment.md)
### Balance
- Balance on difficult and challenging equipment

### Rolling and Turning
- Perform rolling and turning movements under challenging conditions
- Perform rolling or turning movements with a flight phase in a controlled manner

### Swinging
- Perform a sequence of swinging or pendulum movements.
- Perform a sequence of movements under challenging conditions

### Springing, Supporting and Climbing
- Vary the way of overcoming obstacles
- Perform overhead support jumps

### Risk and Responsibility
- Act responsibly towards others and themselves

### Supporting, Securing and Cooperating
- Create and present movement sequences on apparatus combinations in groups

### Flexibilty and Strength
- Explain and apply training principles for improving flexibility and increasing strength
- Independently train flexibility and strength

### Body Tension
- Tense and relax their body at the right moments during movement sequences

## [Performing and Dancing](../Competences/Performing_and_Dancing.md)
### Body Awareness
- Feel themselves during movement execution and make corrections
- Develop an understanding of the connection between control and movement quality

### Performance
- Choreograph and present a movement sequence 

### Movement Skills
- Choreograph and present a sequence of movement tricks
- Teach movement tricks

### Rhytmical Movement
- Interpret different time signatures and musical styles rhythmically
- Recognize the structure of music and create their own movement sequence accordingly

### Dancing
- Memorize and present dance choreographies
- Use the body as a means of expression, performance, and communication

### Respectful Behaviour
- Interact respectfully during creative processes

## [Playing ](../Competences/Playing.md)
### Playing, Developing and Inventing
- Further develop, invent, and play games independently and fairly
- Address and resolve conflicts in games constructively

### Receiving and Passing
- Receive and pass the ball or game object appropriately in game situations

### Leading Balls or Objects
- Control the ball or game object effectively in team sports

### Target Aiming
- Hit a target despite pressure from opponents 

### Tactics
- Apply tactical action patterns in team sports
- Analyze game situations and find solutions

### Rules
- Explain important rules of sports, play independently and fairly
- Address and resolve conflicts during play

### Emotions
- Reflect on emotions independently
- Manage emotions consciously

### Combat
- Use strength and strategy purposefully in combat games

## [Moving in Water](../Competences/Moving_in_Water.md)
### Swimming
- Swim 100 meters in a self-chosen technique
- Swim a long distance using a freely chosen technique
- Name and apply key features of various swimming techniques
- Apply techniques from other aquatic sports

### Jumping into Water and Diving
- Dive head-first into deep water and reach a depth greater than their height
- Perform various jumps from different heights
- Equalize pressure when diving to depth

### Security
- Remind others to follow swimming and diving safety rules
- Act responsibly toward themselves and others
- Name causes of drowning

### Alarming and Rescuing
- Recognize emergencies and take appropriate action
- Apply basic rescue techniques
