# 1st Cycle

##  [Running, Jumping, Throwing](../Competences/Running_Jumping_Throwing.md)
### Fast Running
- Fast running
- Run fast on the balls of the feet
- Start from different positions and run on max speed for short distance

### Endurance Running
- Recognize the own effort and rest periods
- Run intensely again after short recovery breaks
- Describe how effort and recovery feel in the body after intense running

### Orientation
- Navigate independently in the gymnasium and on the school playground
- Orient while running on the school grounds
- Navigate while running using orientation aids

### Rhytmical Jumping
- Hop rhythmically
- Perform various hopping and jumping styles using equipment/materials
- Jump rope

### Long Jumping
- Jump for distance on one leg and both legs
- Take off with the right and left leg when running up

### High Jumping
- Jump vertically on one leg and both legs
- Run up and jump over low obstacles using both the right and left leg for take-off

### Throwing
- Throw objects far
- Throw objects far with both the right and left hand

## [Moving on Gymnastic Equipment](../Competences/Moving_on_Gymnastic_Equipment.md)
### Balance
- Balance on a narrow surface
- Balance on a narrow surface in various ways
- Balance on unstable equipment

### Rolling and Turning
- Roll and turn on an inclined Surface
- Perform a forward roll
- Roll and turn on various apparatus

### Swinging
- Swing on and off different pieces of equipment
- Perceive the turning point during swinging and pendulum movements
- Swing rhythmically on the rings

### Springing, Supporting and Climbing
- Move on apparatus by supporting themselves and swinging
- Land with control
- Jump off a springboard with both feet and land with control
- Overcome various obstacles in a sequence by supporting themselves and climbing

### Risk and Responsibility
- Recognize risky situations and name the emotions
- Reflect on risky situations with guidance

### Supporting, Securing and Cooperating
- Lead each other
- Carry each other correctly and safely
- Name equipment, transport it correctly, and set it up according to a plan

### Flexibilty and Strength
- Perceive the range of motion of their joints
- Utilize the range of motion of their joints and support their body
- Support their tense body on their hands

### Body Tension
- Perceive the body as relaxed and tense
- Tense the body in static I-poses

## [Performing and Dancing](../Competences/Performing_and_Dancing.md)
### Body Awareness
- Perceive the environment and themselves using various senses and distinguish and name body parts
- Perceive the position of the body in space
- Deliberately control body parts

### Performance
- Move to songs, verses, and images, and creatively interpret their content through improvisation
- Imitate movements and experience themselves in different roles
- Express feelings and find own movements to match them

### Movement Skills
- Move an object according to its properties
- Throw and catch an object with both the right and left hand
- Perform movement patterns using various materials

### Rhytmical Movement
- Adapt their movements to the music
- Move with the beat

### Dancing
- Move in contrasting ways
- Move expressively in various dance styles

### Respectful Behaviour
- Move in a group and behave respectfully
- Appreciate the own movement expression

## [Playing ](../Competences/Playing.md)
### Playing, Developing and Inventing
- Recognize given roles in games
- Participate in games in various roles and follow the rules
- Take on different roles during a game and act accordingly

### Receiving and Passing
- Receive and pass objects
- Receive and pass a ball or other game object while running
- Keep the ball or game object in play in small group situations

### Leading Balls or Objects
- Control the ball or game object
- Control the ball or game object next to others

### Target Aiming
- Hit a target from a standing position
- Hit a target while running

### Tactics
- Recognize the movements of teammates and opponents and respond accordingly
- Offer as a passing option and pass to teammates

### Rules
- Name rules
- Follow rules
- Apply rules while playing in small groups

### Emotions
- Perceive own emotions
- Express own emotions and recognize the emotions of others

### Rules
- Accept physical contact
- Recognize and set stop signals
- Name and follow rituals and rules in combat games

## [Sliding, Rolling and Riding](../Competences/Sliding_Rolling_Riding.md)
### Rolling and Riding
- Ride on wheeled devices in a protected and safe environment
- Avoid obstacles and brake safely on wheeled devices

### Sliding
- Slide in various body positions
- Slide in a controlled manner using sliding devices

### Security and Responsibility
- Move safely in nature in different weather and surface conditions
- Follow given safety rules
- Recognize dangerous situations

## [Moving in Water](../Competences/Moving_in_Water.md)
### Swimming
- Move and play freely in chest-deep water
- Apply the core elements of breathing, floating, gliding, and propulsion in various situations
- Stay afloat in place for one minute

### Jumping into Water and Diving
- Jump feet-first into chest-deep water
- Stay underwater briefly and exhale while submerged
- Jump into deep water and submerge completely
- Keep their eyes open underwater and retrieve objects in chest-deep water

### Security
- Name risks in, near, and on the water
- Recognize dangerous situations and follow rules under supervision

### Alarming and Rescuing
- Raise the alarm on instruction
